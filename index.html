The approach

More than back-pain doctors

Chiropractic is a drug-free healthcare profession focused on the musculoskeletal and nervous systems. Your nervous system — brain, spinal cord, and the nerves branching to every cell, tissue, and organ — just happens to be housed inside your spine.

We’re highly specialized in analyzing joint mobility and correcting subluxations (misalignments) in the spine, using gentle techniques that restore proper joint function and, more importantly, nervous system communication. We get to the root cause — and the symptoms begin to go away on their own.

Know what to expect before you walk in
